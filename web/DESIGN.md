# Arrivow visual system

User selected direction C: the dark-and-amber conference venue board.

- Graphite #131615 page with raised #1d211f cards; ivory #f0eee6 text; amber #edc34f for actions, dates and active navigation, with a soft amber tint for highlighted surfaces. Subdued text remains readable against graphite.
- Barlow Condensed for programme headings, dates, and the Arrivow wordmark; Public Sans for prose, forms, and controls. Tabular numerals for amounts and time.
- Content sits in a centred 1200px column. Discovery opens with a short hero and a three-step "Commit, check in, claim" card, then a search/filter bar and event cards in a grid: upcoming first, past events dimmed below. Narrow screens become one column.
- Event cards have an amber-tinted band with the date and deposit, then title, time, location and seats. Detail, host, ticket, organizer and networking screens use the same raised, rounded cards (14px radius, soft shadow). No glowing accents. Buttons have rounded corners and ample hit areas.
- Every payment displays the network, refundable deposit, non-refundable storage fee, and settlement timing. Preview content must be explicitly labelled.
- Ticket, organizer, and networking screens inherit the same signage language and remain usable on a phone at the venue.
