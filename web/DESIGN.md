# Arrivow visual system

User selected direction C: the dark-and-amber conference venue board.

- Graphite #171a19 background; ivory #f0eee6 text; amber #edc34f for actions and active navigation. Subdued text remains readable against graphite.
- Barlow Condensed for programme headings, dates, and the Arrivow wordmark; Public Sans for prose, forms, and controls. Tabular numerals for amounts and time.
- Desktop discovery uses an anchored left-hand statement and filters, with a chronological ruled programme on the right. Narrow screens become one reading column.
- Flat surfaces and thin dividers; no gradients, glowing accents, or floating dashboard cards. Buttons have modest radii and ample hit areas.
- Every payment displays the network, refundable deposit, non-refundable storage fee, and settlement timing. Preview content must be explicitly labelled.
- Ticket, organizer, and networking screens inherit the same signage language and remain usable on a phone at the venue.
