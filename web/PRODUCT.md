# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Conference organizers managing sessions and side events; attendees committing to attendance and meeting relevant people. The first release supports at most 200 active registrations per event.

## Product Purpose

Reduce no-shows through refundable attendance deposits. Checked-in attendees receive their deposit plus a published share of no-show deposits; organizers receive the remainder. Make the full flow demonstrable for NEAR HACKATHON.

## Operating Context

Hosts create events on desktop and check guests in on phones. Guests browse without a wallet, connect an existing NEAR wallet to register, present a short-lived QR ticket, and claim their payout. Optional cross-chain funding uses 1Click. Optional matchmaking ranks shared interests within the application service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Native NEAR deposits; immutable event terms; cancellation before the published cutoff; permissionless settlement; host-attested attendance. No ticket sales, platform fee, account creation, or gas sponsorship. App service can read opt-in networking profiles; profiles never go onchain. Testnet and mainnet are visibly distinct.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Arrivow. Tagline: Showing up pays. User chose an image mockup before interface implementation. No legacy Court branding is retained.

## Evidence on Hand

No deployed event contract, customer claims, or live event fixtures exist yet. Any preview event must be labeled as illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Make deposit rules clear before a signature.
- Keep money controlled by the contract.
- Make host check-in fast and recoverable.
- Keep networking optional and contacts subject to mutual consent.
- Expose network, pending actions, and errors honestly.
